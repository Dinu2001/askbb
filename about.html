Our Legacy

A century of musical excellence and cultural heritage at Ananda Sastralaya Kotte.

A Journey Through Time

Ananda Sastralaya, Kotte, established in 1890, is one of the most respected educational institutions in Sri Lanka. Rooted in strong cultural and academic traditions, the school has played a significant role in shaping generations of students who have gone on to contribute meaningfully to society.

Among its many proud traditions, the Western Band of Ananda Sastralaya holds a special place. Known for its discipline, precision, and musical excellence, the band has become a symbol of pride for the school. Over the years, it has performed at major school events, national celebrations, and inter-school competitions.

Today, Ananda Sastralaya continues to uphold its legacy by nurturing not only academic excellence but also musical talent, ensuring that the spirit of tradition and innovation lives on through every performance and every generation.
