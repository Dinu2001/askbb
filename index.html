About the Chapter

A Night the Soul Remembers

Rhythm of Sastra is more than a concert it is a tribute to the rich musical heritage of Ananda Sastralaya, hand curated by its Old Boys Association. Every chapter invites Sri Lanka's finest voices and instrumentalists to share a stage that celebrates tradition, brotherhood, and the timeless embrace of rhythm and soul.

What to Expect

Highlights of Chapter 04

Live Musical Showcase

A meticulously curated lineup of vocalists, instrumentalists and guest artists performing across multiple moods from the classical to the contemporary.

An Auditorium Experience

Premium seating across Ground Floor and Balcony tiers, thoughtfully designed so every row enjoys uncompromised sightlines and sound.

A Tradition Since 2019

Chapter 04 continues a tradition that began as an intimate alumni gathering and has grown into one of Colombo's most anticipated cultural evenings.
